
**Note:** The seed is idempotent - it can run multiple times safely.

## 7. Find Backend URL

After deployment, Railway provides a URL for your backend service.

1. Go to your backend service in Railway
2. Click on the **"Settings"** tab
3. Find the **"Networking"** section
4. Your URL will be listed as:
   ```
   https://nexora-backend-production.up.railway.app
   ```
5. Copy this URL - you'll need it for testing and frontend config
